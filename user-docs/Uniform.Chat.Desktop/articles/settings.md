# Settings

*⋮ → Settings* at the top of the room list sets the theme (follow the system, light or dark), the text size, and which notifications to show; see Notifications. Changes are saved right away.

## Audio test

*Test audio…* in the settings plays your microphone back a second later, through the Opus codec that calls use, to try the microphone and the speaker before a call. Choose *Start*, speak, and listen; *Stop* shows how long it recorded and at what bit rate. Silence means the input is not a microphone: choose one in the system's sound settings.

On phones, the switch chooses the loudspeaker or the earpiece; with the earpiece, hold the phone to your ear. The first start asks for permission to record audio.

## Logs

*Logs…* in the settings shows what the app has logged: the session's connection, sync, encryption and calls, as in the terminal client's log window. The list at the top chooses the session, the newest marked *(now)*; the level chooses how much detail; the filter shows only entries with the text. Newest entries come first. *Refresh* reads the log again, and *Copy* copies the shown entries, e.g. to send with a bug report. The logs stay on the device.

## Licences

*Licences…* at the bottom of the settings shows the license of Uniform Chat and of everything the app includes: the .NET runtime and each library, with its copyright notice. Choose one in the list to read it; *‹* goes back to the settings, as does the back button on Android.
